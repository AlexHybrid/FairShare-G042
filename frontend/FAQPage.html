Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have compiled answers to the most common queries about FairShare. Click on any question to view the answer.

What is FairShare? ▼
FairShare is a smart, easy-to-use house rental management and bill splitting application. It helps roommates log shared house expenses, split them automatically, visualize the split via charts, and export data.
How does the bill splitting calculation work? ▼
The system aggregates all added expenses and groups them by room or housemate. The total expense is visualized in the dashboard donut chart. Roommates can track exactly who owes what in the dues table.
How do I add a new expense? ▼
You can add an expense by clicking the "Add Expense" button on the dashboard or "Add Expenses" in the sidebar. This opens a form where you enter the date, select the room/housemate, enter the expense type, amount, category, status, and upload a receipt.
Can I upload receipts for bill payments? ▼
Yes, when adding or saving an expense, there is an optional "Receipt Upload" field where you can upload receipt images or PDFs (e.g. JPG, PNG, PDF) to keep a clear record of payment.
How do I export bill reports? ▼
Go to the "Export Data" section from the sidebar or click "Export Data" under the Landlord section. You can choose to download the live report as either an Excel file (.xlsx) or a PDF report (.pdf).
