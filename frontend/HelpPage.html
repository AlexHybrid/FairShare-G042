How to start using FairShare

Follow these simple steps to manage your house rental and split bills effortlessly.

Quick System Demo

1

Familiarize with the Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your central hub. Here you can view the Donut Chart for a visual breakdown of expenses by room, and check the Dues Table to see a detailed list of all logged bills, their amounts, and current payment status (Pending or Paid).

2

Add a New Expense

Click the Add Expense button on the Dashboard. A form will appear where you can enter the Date, select the Room/Housemate responsible, specify the Expense Type (e.g. Electricity), the Amount, Category, and Payment Method. You can also upload a receipt photo or PDF for proof. Click "Save Expense" when done.

3

Calculate Valid Split Breakdown

Wondering who owes who? Click the Valid Split button next to the "Add Expense" button. FairShare will automatically divide the total expenses by the number of rooms and show you a clean breakdown of exactly how much each room owes or receives to settle the bills fairly.

4

Manage Rental Agreements & Payments

From the right sidebar Menu, you can navigate to the Agreement page to view and digitally sign your tenancy agreement using the built-in signature pad. You can also use the Payment page to log your monthly rent payments safely.

5

Landlord Features & Exporting Reports

The Landlord Section is secured by a private master PIN. Once unlocked, the landlord can post global Announcements that all tenants will see. They can also navigate to the Export Data page to download a full record of expenses in Excel (.xlsx) or PDF format for offline keeping.
