How to start using FairShare

Follow these simple steps to manage your house rental and split bills effortlessly.

1

Add Your First Expense

Navigate to the Dashboard and click the Add Expense button. Fill in the details of the bill such as the date, amount, category (e.g. Utilities, Rent), and which room or housemate it belongs to. You can even upload a receipt for proof!

2

Track Split Bills Automatically

Once you add expenses, FairShare does the math for you. The donut chart on the Dashboard will update to show you the visual breakdown, and the dues table will list exactly who owes what, keeping everything transparent.

3

Settle Up & Update Status

When a housemate pays their share, you can mark the expense status as Paid in the system. Keeping track of pending and paid statuses helps avoid any confusion at the end of the month.

4

Export Monthly Reports

At the end of the month, go to the Export Data section from the side menu. You can easily download a comprehensive summary in PDF or Excel format to share with your landlord or keep for your personal records.
