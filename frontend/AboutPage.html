About FairShare

FairShare is a modern, premium house rental and bill splitting solution designed to bring transparency and harmony to shared living spaces. Whether you're managing room agreements, tracking rental payments, or splitting monthly utilities, FairShare makes roommate collaboration effortless, clean, and fast.

🏠 Rent Tracker

Easily manage room listings, track monthly rental agreements, and check payment status in real-time.

💸 Smart Splitting

Split utilities, electricity, water, internet, and food expenses dynamically with automatic dues calculations.

📈 Visual Analytics

Understand your household expenses immediately through our clean, interactive visual charts and details.

📄 Export Reports

Generate and export comprehensive Excel spreadsheets or PDF summaries for landlords or personal records.
